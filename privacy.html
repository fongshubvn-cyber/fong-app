Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Fong Auto Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What data the app uses

  • TikTok access tokens for the owner's own account, used only to upload videos to that account's drafts.
  • Video files that the owner chooses to upload, read from the owner's own storage device.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private storage device. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from any other TikTok users.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings, which invalidates all stored tokens.
